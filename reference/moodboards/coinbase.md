# Coinbase

## Why it's a reference

A second exchange/trading-platform reference alongside Kraken, useful
specifically for its **institutional-side sub-brand** (Coinbase
Prime/Institutional) — closer in audience to Nelogica's broker/prop-firm
personas than Coinbase's retail app marketing.

## What to steal

- **Institutional page built entirely around trust and infrastructure
  claims** (custody, security, liquidity, compliance) stated as plain
  fact with minimal adjectives — the clearest working example of
  DESIGN.md's "confidence comes from showing real capability... not
  from hype language."
- **Market data (live-feeling price tickers, asset lists) used as
  ambient texture** in section backgrounds at low contrast — a way to
  keep "market data" visually present throughout the page without
  every section being a literal chart screenshot.
- **Clean separation between retail and institutional visual systems**
  on the same domain — same brand marks, deliberately different
  density/tone per audience, reinforcing that persona-routing (trader
  vs. broker vs. prop firm) can shift emphasis without breaking brand
  cohesion (DESIGN.md's "one visual identity across sections"
  principle still applies at the token/type level even as content
  density shifts).

## What NOT to copy

- Coinbase's retail consumer app marketing (bright, playful,
  illustration-heavy) is the wrong register entirely for Nelogica —
  reference the institutional/Prime pages only, not coinbase.com's
  general consumer homepage.

## Where to look

- Coinbase Prime / Coinbase Institutional pages specifically, not the
  general consumer Coinbase.com homepage or app store pages.
