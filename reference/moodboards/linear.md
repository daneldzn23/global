# Linear

## Why it's a reference

The clearest counter-example to the exact "generic AI-landing-page
grammar" DESIGN.md names outright (rainbow-highlight headlines,
thin-line eyebrow tags, glass/blur cards, numbered 01/02/03
scaffolding). Linear is B2B software marketing that deliberately
avoids every item on that list, at dark-mode, technical-audience scale
— the closest tonal match to Nelogica's own palette family.

## What to steal

- **No eyebrow tags, no rainbow-highlight headlines.** Section
  headings are plain, confident, single-weight typography — direct
  fix for the two most-repeated V1.4 patterns DESIGN.md calls out by
  name.
- **Dark UI screenshots with sharp edges and real drop shadows**,
  never `backdrop-filter: blur()` glass cards — solid surfaces,
  crisp borders, shadow used for depth instead of translucency for
  decoration.
- **Sequential product reveals via scroll-triggered state changes**
  (a UI panel updates/highlights as you scroll past it) instead of a
  static feature-icon grid — supports DESIGN.md's "motion has a job"
  principle: motion reveals real product behavior, not decoration.
- **Asymmetric section layouts** — text block and product visual
  rarely centered/mirrored the same way twice, avoiding the
  interchangeable-card-grid sameness DESIGN.md flags as a risk across
  Nelogica's Recursos/Corretoras/Multiplataforma sections.

## What NOT to copy

- Linear's extreme minimalism (very sparse copy, lots of negative
  space, single-product focus) doesn't fit Nelogica's requirement to
  stay information-dense across multiple products (BlackArrow/Bookmap/
  Profit/OMS) and multiple personas — take the anti-template
  typography/motion discipline, not the sparseness.

## Where to look

- linear.app homepage — specifically how each feature section handles
  the heading/visual pairing without repeating the same eyebrow+card
  formula twice in a row.
